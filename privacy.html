Privacy Policy — Bakery Rush

Last updated: September 2026

Bakery Rush is a single-player arcade game. This policy explains, plainly, what happens with your information when you play.

No data is collected

Bakery Rush does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, device identifiers, or usage information. The game does not require an account, does not connect to any server, and does not use analytics or tracking of any kind.

No advertising

The game contains no advertisements and no ad networks are integrated into it.

No third parties

Bakery Rush does not share information with third parties, because it does not collect any information to share.

How the game works

All gameplay — your score, progress, and settings — exists only in your device's memory while you're playing. Nothing is saved to a server, and nothing persists after you close the app beyond what your device itself may cache for normal app operation.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future update adds features that do collect data — this page will be updated to reflect that before the update ships.
